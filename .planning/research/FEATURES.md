# Feature Research — School Profile Website

## Table Stakes

- School identity profile (name, accreditation, address, contacts)
- Vision & mission, history, leadership summary
- Programs/majors and facilities pages
- News/announcements publishing
- Gallery/documentation section
- Responsive mobile layout and clear navigation

## Differentiators

- Rich achievement timeline and spotlight sections
- Event calendar with filtered categories
- Structured admissions information journey
- Download center (brochures, forms, guides)

## Anti-Features (for v1)

- Student grading/attendance system integration
- Complex parent/student portal workflows
- Native mobile apps

## Complexity Notes

- Table stakes are low-medium complexity and ideal for early phases.
- Differentiators are medium complexity and should follow stable content/admin baseline.
